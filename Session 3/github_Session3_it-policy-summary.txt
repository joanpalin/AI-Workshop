IT POLICY SUMMARY — INFORMATION SECURITY AND ACCEPTABLE USE

Owner: Head of IT Operations

1. ACCEPTABLE USE OF INFORMATION SYSTEMS

1.1 ARK Industries information systems — including network infrastructure, workstations, email, collaboration platforms, and cloud services — are provided for business use. Incidental personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with business operations, consume excessive resources, or create reputational or legal risk for the company.

1.2 Employees must not use company systems to access, store, or distribute material that is unlawful, offensive, or contrary to company values. This includes but is not limited to: content that violates copyright, discriminatory or harassing material, and content that facilitates unauthorised access to third-party systems.

1.3 The use of personal cloud storage services (including consumer-grade services such as personal Dropbox, Google Drive, or iCloud accounts) for storage or transfer of company data is prohibited.

1.4 All use of company systems is subject to monitoring in accordance with applicable law and the ARK Industries Employee Privacy Notice. Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company-provided systems or networks.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

2. DEVICE MANAGEMENT AND ENDPOINT SECURITY

2.1 All endpoints accessing ARK Industries systems must be enrolled in the company's Mobile Device Management (MDM) platform prior to use. Unenrolled devices are blocked at the network perimeter.

2.2 Operating system and application security patches must be applied within 30 days of release for critical and high-severity patches, and within 90 days for medium-severity patches.

2.3 Full-disk encryption is mandatory on all company-issued laptops and mobile devices. Encryption status is monitored and enforced via MDM.

2.4 Antivirus and endpoint detection software approved by the IT Security team must be installed and active on all endpoints. Employees must not disable or modify security software.

2.5 Personal devices (BYOD) may access company email and calendar via the approved mobile email client only. Personal devices must not be connected to the internal network via wired or wireless access without prior approval from IT Operations.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3. SOFTWARE INSTALLATION POLICY

3.1 Employees are not permitted to install software on company devices without prior approval from IT Operations. All software requests must be submitted via the IT Service Desk using the Software Approval Request form.

3.2 Approved software is distributed via the company's software deployment platform. Employees must not circumvent this process by downloading and installing applications directly from the internet.

3.3 Open-source software may only be used where it has been reviewed and approved by IT Operations and, where applicable, the Legal team. The use of unapproved open-source components in company systems or projects is prohibited.

3.4 Browser extensions and plug-ins are subject to the same approval requirements as standalone software. Unapproved extensions with access to browsing data or clipboard contents are explicitly prohibited.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4. REMOTE ACCESS

4.1 Remote access to ARK Industries internal systems is permitted only via the company-approved Virtual Private Network (VPN) client. The use of third-party VPN services or split-tunnelling configurations not sanctioned by IT is prohibited.

4.2 Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all remote access connections. Employees must not share or transfer authentication tokens or one-time codes with any other person.

4.3 Remote access sessions must not be conducted from unsecured public networks (such as open Wi-Fi in cafes, hotels, or airports) unless the VPN is active for the duration of the session.

4.4 Employees working remotely are responsible for ensuring that their home network is appropriately secured and that unauthorised persons do not have access to the employee's screen or device during a work session.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5. DATA CLASSIFICATION AND HANDLING

5.1 ARK Industries data is classified into four tiers: Public, Internal Use Only, Confidential, and Restricted. Data handling obligations are determined by classification tier and are detailed in the ARK Industries Data Classification Standard (ARK-IT-STD-004).

5.2 Confidential and Restricted data must not be transmitted via unencrypted channels. All email containing Confidential or Restricted data must use TLS-encrypted delivery. Where TLS cannot be confirmed, end-to-end encrypted messaging must be used.

5.3 Confidential and Restricted data must not be stored on removable media unless the media is encrypted and the transfer has been approved in writing by the employee's line manager and the Head of IT Operations.

5.4 Data retention periods are defined in the ARK Industries Records Retention Schedule (ARK-LEGAL-001). Employees must not retain data beyond the applicable retention period. Disposal of physical media containing Confidential or Restricted data must follow the secure disposal procedure.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6. INCIDENT REPORTING

6.1 Employees must report any actual or suspected information security incident to the IT Security team immediately upon discovery. Reports must be made via the IT Security Incident form on the intranet or, where the intranet is inaccessible, by telephone to the IT Security Hotline.

6.2 A security incident includes but is not limited to: suspected malware infection, accidental disclosure of Confidential or Restricted data, loss or theft of a company device, phishing emails that have been clicked or responded to, and unauthorised access to company systems or data.

6.3 Employees must not attempt to investigate, remediate, or contain a security incident without the involvement and direction of the IT Security team. Unauthorised remediation attempts may destroy forensic evidence and can worsen the impact of an incident.

6.4 Incident response timelines and escalation procedures are detailed in the ARK Industries Incident Response Plan (ARK-IT-IRP-001). The plan is reviewed annually.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

POLICY COMPLIANCE

Compliance with this policy is mandatory for all ARK Industries employees, contractors, and third parties with access to company systems. Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including dismissal, and may be referred to relevant authorities where a violation constitutes a criminal offence.

Questions regarding this policy should be directed to the IT Operations team via the IT Service Desk.
